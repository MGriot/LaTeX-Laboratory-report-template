\section*{Hazard and precautionary statements}

If you prefer not to write the full H and P statements in the reagent tables, list them here. Complete lists are available from \href{https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ghs/}{PubChem} and \href{https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/clp/labelling}{ECHA}.
